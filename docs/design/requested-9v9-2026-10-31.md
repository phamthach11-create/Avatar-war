## User-confirmed summon/trigger rules
- One existing Automaton of each type per team, across all summoners; no duplicate cast cost when capped.
- Automata occupy no 9v9 slot and cannot be targeted. They disappear immediately when their summoner dies. Other Leonardo can then resummon that type.
- Only Base Attack inherits at 50%+10%*SL. Base MHR/Accuracy=100%; CHC/PEN/CDB=0; selected automaton upgrades apply. No ordinary Leonardo combat stat inheritance.
- Fire at the start of allied team turn, random valid enemy, Piercer/Devastator weapon area and penalties. New summons begin attacking at the next allied start, not immediately during the summon action. Owner stun does not disable the independent summoned attacker.
- Grand Battery auto-casts at allied turn start when ready and MP sufficient; all eligible Napoleons receive Rage/Onslaught and the selected healing upgrade.
- Main death during an automatic start effect ends the fight immediately, before AP/slots.
